Hi! I'm Ellie. 

I'm currently a PhD candidate at the University of Connecticut, working in the Heyduk Lab. Here, you'll find a growing collection of my research, art, and educational materials. 

Plants perform photosynthesis to convert light energy into chemical energy. 

At its core, photosynthesis is a series of reactions. In stressful environments, some plants can alter these reactions to be more efficient; for example, Crassulacean Acid Metabolism  (CAM) is a photosynthetic pathway where plants take up carbon dioxide at nighttime and store it as acid intermediates to be turned into sugars the following day.

My research in the Heyduk Lab is looking at how aquatic plants photosynthesize among the challenges of the aquatic habitat, and in a changing environmental landscape.
